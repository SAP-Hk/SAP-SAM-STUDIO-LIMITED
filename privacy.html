Rillday Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026. This policy describes the Android app Rillday (com.rillday.planner). Privacy contact: pha.nduc7370p@gmail.com.

Local data

Rillday stores task titles, notes, dates, times, durations, priorities, completion states, reminder delivery records and preferences in private app storage on your device. These are used to display your schedule and deliver reminders. Rillday does not operate a server, create accounts, use advertising or analytics, or transmit this data to the developer. The app does not request internet access.

Reminders and permissions

Notifications are optional. On supported Android versions, you can grant or deny notification permission. Task titles and times may appear in notifications; your Android lock-screen settings control their visibility. The app receives device restart and clock/time-zone change events to reschedule reminders. Delivery may be delayed by Android battery restrictions. No access to contacts, location, microphone, camera or broad storage is requested.

Exports and backups

You can export a complete JSON backup or a CSV using Android’s system file picker. You choose the destination and who can access it. Files contain the data you export and are not encrypted by Rillday. A cloud file provider chosen by you may upload exported files under its own policy. Importing a valid backup replaces your local schedule only after confirmation. Automatic Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete tasks, replace the schedule through restore, clear app storage through Android settings, or uninstall Rillday. These actions do not remove previously exported files. Delete exported copies separately through your chosen storage provider. There is no Rillday account or server-side account deletion process.

Security and third-party components

Rillday relies on Android app-private storage and device security. It does not add its own password or encryption layer. Keep your device and exported files secure. AndroidX and Kotlin libraries provide interface and runtime functionality; no advertising, analytics or cloud SDK is integrated.

Support and children

If you contact support by email, your email service sends your address and the information you choose to include. Support messages are used to respond to your request; do not send unnecessary sensitive information. You can request deletion of support correspondence by contacting the address above, subject to applicable record-retention obligations. Rillday does not knowingly collect children’s data through the app.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when app behavior changes. Review the effective date. Questions and privacy requests: pha.nduc7370p@gmail.com.
